Questions

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Coo is signed by its developer and checked by Apple (“notarized”), so your Mac opens it without warnings. Passwords and sign-ins are kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Does it cost anything?

No. Coo is free.

Google says “Google hasn't verified this app”. Is that OK?

Yes. Coo is a small independent app that hasn't been through Google's paid review yet, so Google shows that notice when you add a Gmail account. Click Advanced, then Go to Coo, and allow access. Coo only talks to Google from your Mac; your mail never goes anywhere else. Microsoft and iCloud accounts don't show this.

Which accounts work?

Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com, Gmail and Google Workspace, and iCloud. Add as many as you have: one per business, a personal one, a family one.

Can my AI assistant use all my accounts?

Yes. Open Coo, choose Help › Connect an AI Agent, and copy the one line it shows (or click “Add to Claude Code”). Your assistant then sees every account you've added to Coo through that one connection. By default it can only read and search; it can only send, archive or answer invites if you allow it, and even then replies start as drafts. Developers: run coo agent-help.

Can my assistant keep an eye on my mail for me?

With Claude Code, yes: ask it to watch for a reply on a conversation, mail from someone, or a subject, and it's told the moment that mail arrives in Coo. You can ask it to stop at any time.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Coo checks for new versions quietly and installs them when you restart it, so you're always up to date.

What does Coo see of my mail?

Only your Mac sees it. Coo keeps a copy on your computer so it's fast, and talks directly to your email provider. Crash reports, if something goes wrong, are anonymous: addresses and message text are removed before they leave your Mac, and you can switch them off in Settings. Privacy details.

How do I remove it?

In Coo, open Settings and sign out of your accounts, then drag Coo from Applications to the Trash.
